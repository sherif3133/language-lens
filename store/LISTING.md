# Chrome Web Store listing: Language Lens – Sawa English

## Store listing

**Name:** Language Lens – Sawa English (from the manifest)

**Summary** (132 characters max; the manifest description is used):
Learn English from YouTube and every website: meanings on hover, word levels, pronunciation, a writing checker and review cards.

**Category:** Education
**Language:** English

**Description:**

Language Lens turns the English you already read and watch into lessons. It's made for Sawa English members (Arabic speakers learning English at A2–B1), and it works with your free Sawa English account.

ON ANY WEBSITE
• Hover a word for its meaning in Arabic (or simple English), its level (A1–C2), pronunciation and how it sounds in real speech.
• Words above your level get a soft colour, so you see at a glance whether a page is right for you.
• Select a phrase to translate it, hear it, or save it to your review cards.
• 💡 Curious: hover a word you just want to understand and earn XP for being curious, without adding it to your reviews.
• Arabic → English: hover an Arabic word to see it in English, or select Arabic text to read it in English.
• Links, menus and buttons are left alone, so the page stays clean.

ON YOUTUBE
• Clickable English captions with an Arabic line and a "how it sounds" line.
• A full transcript next to the video, with the 20 most used words and how hard the video is for your level.
• Go back, repeat or skip a sentence with one key (A / S / D) to practise your listening and pronunciation.

WRITE BETTER
• Spelling mistakes are underlined while you type in any text box, and grammar mistakes after a short pause. Click one to fix it.

LEARN EVERY DAY
• New words come back as review cards in the Sawa English app before you forget them.
• A daily limit on new words keeps reviews light.
• See how many English words you read and heard each day.

Your data: pages are read on your device. Only text you ask about (and text you write, for the grammar check) is sent to Sawa English. No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. Privacy policy: https://sawaenglish.com/language-lens-privacy/

## Privacy practices tab

**Single purpose:**
Helps English learners understand and learn the English words they meet on websites and YouTube (meanings, pronunciation, levels, saving words for review, and checking their own writing).

**Permission justifications:**
- **storage**: Stores the learner's word list, settings and the built-in dictionary on the device so meanings show instantly and work offline.
- **identity**: Opens the Sawa English sign-in page (launchWebAuthFlow) so the learner can connect their account; no Google account data is read.
- **scripting**: Runs the word-meaning tools on the page the learner is reading when it was opened before the extension was installed or updated.
- **activeTab**: Lets the learner turn Language Lens on or pause it for the current page from the toolbar icon.
- **alarms**: Syncs saved words and review cards with the learner's Sawa English account in the background every few minutes.
- **Host permission (all http/https sites, content scripts)**: The core feature is showing meanings and word levels for the English text on whatever page the learner is reading, and checking what they write in text boxes. Page text is analysed on the device; only text the learner asks about (or types, for grammar checks) is sent to sawaenglish.com.
- **youtube.com**: Shows interactive captions and a transcript next to YouTube videos.
- **sawaenglish.com**: Syncs the learner's words, sentences and review cards with their account.

**Remote code:** No, I am not using remote code. (All scripts are in the package; the server returns only data (JSON).)

**Data usage (tick these):**
- ☑ Personally identifiable information: name and email of the Sawa English account (for sign-in).
- ☑ Authentication information: a sign-in token for the Sawa English account.
- ☑ User activity: words the learner looks up and saves, and the number of words read and heard each day.
- ☑ Website content: text the learner selects or asks about, and text typed into text boxes for grammar checks.
- ☐ Health, financial, location, web history, personal communications: not collected.

**Certifications (tick all three):**
- I do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, outside of the approved use cases.
- I do not use or transfer user data for purposes that are unrelated to my item's single purpose.
- I do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
